What is Kheti Kranti?

Kheti Kranti is an online platform where farmers can find farm equipment to rent and list equipment they are not using. Discover nearby machinery, compare listings and manage bookings in one place.

Explore the marketplace →
01

Our purpose

Making farm equipment easier to access and put to work.

Farm machinery is expensive, and much of it sits unused for much of the year. Kheti Kranti connects farmers who need equipment with owners who have it, helping farmers save on purchases while owners earn from equipment that might otherwise stay idle.

03

Frequently asked questions

More about using Kheti Kranti.

Search for farm equipment, review listings and book what you need. If you own equipment, list it for sale or rent and manage orders through your account.

Your location can help sort equipment by distance and show listings near you. Location is optional; you can deny permission and continue browsing.

Payment options depend on each listing. Sellers may accept online payment, cash on delivery or both; available methods are shown before you place an order.

04

The impact we hope to make

Helping equipment work for more people.

  • Farmers can use modern machinery without having to buy it.
  • Owners can earn from equipment that might otherwise sit idle.
  • Finding equipment nearby can reduce travel and transport costs.
  • More equipment and opportunity can stay within the local farming community.
